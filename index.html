Now flying through the building, not just over it.

FPV (first-person view) drones are flown through goggles in real time. Fast, close and immersive, like walking the space yourself.

Instead of a slow orbit from above, the camera glides through a doorway, banks around a building or chases alongside the action, all in one continuous shot.

It gives venue tours, progress updates and highlight reels the kind of energy a standard drone shot can't.

  • Venue walk-throughs
  • Construction progress
  • Event highlight reels
  • Car and product reveals
